# Govern exceptions and acceptance, not routine programming

Normative for planning, implementation, repair and verification. Apply the current
approved scope and existing authorization, staging, execution, budget and evidence
contracts. This reference supplies no new permission, policy or host capability.

## Default: normal programming inside approved boundaries

Agent chooses routine implementation methods within approved scope without separate
approval, decision records, step-by-step narration or method-change ledgers. Specify
required outcomes and acceptance evidence, not an exhaustive account of how to code.
Selecting among unspecified equivalent methods is not itself a protected-plan amendment.
An expressly fixed requirement remains binding; do not relabel its change as routine.

Plans identify outcomes, scope, required validation and material escalation triggers.
Shared instructions must remain accessible and complete wherever the governing host
requires fully materialized task plans. Preserve original acceptance coverage and
actual independent review requirements, not a mandatory per-work-family checklist.
[Optional examples](implementation-autonomy-examples.md) illustrate this default;
they add no obligations and need not be enumerated in task plans or review reports.

## Escalate actual exceptions and material boundary changes

Use the applicable approval/amendment route when a choice would change:
- Acceptance criteria, required behavior/coverage, fixed requirements or failure waivers.
- Approved task/repair/execution scope, tier, policy, governing tooling or resource limits.
- Safety, privacy, compatibility, persistence, ownership, recovery or completion boundaries.

Unavailable authority, capability or required evidence blocks only the dependent
action. Continue independent permitted work and prepare the specific next proposal.
Changing an explicitly constrained method or a protected executable plan follows
its existing amendment rules; retain the actual decision and renew affected bindings.
Do not require these change records for routine choices that leave those rules intact.

## Verify outcomes and retain required evidence

Ordinary app edits inside approved scope do not require renewed unchanged tier
authorization, but invalidate affected prior run evidence. Validate changed inputs
through the applicable authorized route. Changed governing runner/checker/wrapper,
tier or policy versions still require their applicable review and binding before use.

Code/test authorship does not imply execution authority. Preserve explicit inert
staging exclusions and task-wide gates; authorized execution still requires its
actual scope, finite budgets and independent supervision. Required run, approval,
incident, provenance and acceptance records remain mandatory; no programming diary
or routine per-method approval/ledger is added. Scoped repairs retain applicable
independent post-repair acceptance, not review of every coding step.

Exactly matching tasks may reuse a current verified already-approved deterministic
policy after verified integration and authorized cutover, with a fresh bound decision
for each. No repeated human policy approval is needed. Unmatched/uncertain tasks or
changed policy terms need the applicable decision; this reference activates nothing.
