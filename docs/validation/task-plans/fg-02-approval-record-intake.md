# Capture Source Stage Approval

<!-- regression-guard: not-applicable -->

## What & Why

Capture and verify an authentic current operation-specific decision for the proposed FG-02-I adapter, schema, and regression-case source stage in Skills Workbench. This is evidence intake and documentation only, not an approval action, route implementation, or permission to stage sources.

The proposed subject is installation.bootstrap-admission-contract-stage, one source-authoring bounded capability, empty parameters, and an exact finalized plan/version/digest and canonical/host source closure. The label is a proposal, not a registered capability. A platform task reference is not a local Failure Gate ID.

## Done looks like

- The responsible decision source and a documented supported route applicable to this exact source-stage operation are identified with their version and permitted read scope.
- An existing real decision is verified for exact operation, project-local namespace/ID, plan/version/digest, canonical and host source closure, one bounded capability, parameters, transitive scope, safety constraints, validity/expiry, revocation, and stable source evidence reference.
- Any binding discrepancy, unavailable route, unauthorized read, missing record, or conflicting historical evidence is BLOCKED, not substituted with ordinary plan review, the health-only bootstrap receipt, task approval, or caller claims.
- The report distinguishes a verified record from a narrative/fixture/copy. Authentic evidence remains at its original authority source; the dossier is only an index and assessment.
- No prerequisite is claimed satisfied until a matching current record is verified. If unavailable, deliver the smallest exact owner decision/read-access request and retain the blocker; do not claim approval captured.

## Out of scope

Invoking ordinary review or bootstrap capture; creating approval receipts or approved flags; assigning or allocating local IDs; implementing an approval adapter or deterministic policy; changing the current route, capability manifest, shared handoff, policy, or coordinator; granting authority; source staging; imports, syntax checks, tests, builds, CI, services, signals, cutover, deployment, or task-status changes.

## Steps

1. **Reconfirm the exact subject** — Read permitted current source documents and establish the finalized proposed operation, plan/source bindings and required decision fields. Preserve UNKNOWN governance and distinguish historical stage observations from current proof.
2. **Locate the real decision source** — Identify the responsible source and its supported applicable route without invoking a review or assuming ordinary-plan applicability. Obtain only the specific authorized read/export needed, and stop if that permission or route is absent.
3. **Verify supplied evidence** — Compare the real record with the entire exact subject, validity and revocation evidence. Reject stale, self-authored, unrelated-domain or unverifiable records without modifying their source.
4. **Publish a bounded intake dossier** — Write a task-specific report under tracked validation documentation with source revisions, access permission basis, binding results, limitations, and FOUND/BLOCKED/UNKNOWN status. If blocked, draft the exact decision needed to establish a supported route; do not implement it.

## Task identity and coordination

This task may plan and document only; its platform acceptance creates no local authority. Do not allocate a local ID under this task. Use a genuine source-issued local identity only if already available and verifiable.

The three intake tasks are independent and may collect evidence in parallel against the same finalized subject. They must not edit each other's reports or shared governance documents. Changed subject/source bindings invalidate affected intake conclusions; no automatic Task 96 unblocking or FG-02-V execution follows.

## Pre-existing failures to ignore

None authorized. Preserve unresolved failures and conflicting historical bootstrap evidence without waiver or reclassification.

## Validation

**Command:** None authorized; bounded documentary readback only.
**Why:** This task collects authority evidence and cannot create it. Verify content and source bindings by permitted reads, not imports, scripts, plan guards or tests.
**Do not escalate:** Missing read permission, applicable route, authentic decision or exact bindings means BLOCKED. A proposal or local copy cannot make a decision current.
