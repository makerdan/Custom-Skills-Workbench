# Capture Budget Accounting Evidence

<!-- regression-guard: not-applicable -->

## What & Why

Capture an authentic current finite grant and supported authoritative accounting-read route for the proposed FG-02-I source-stage operation. Policy constants are safe-shape ceilings, not current grants; historical bootstrap allowances do not transfer.

This task is read-only evidence intake with task-specific documentation, not budget issuance, consumption, renewal, route implementation, or validation permission.

## Done looks like

- The responsible budget source, source/version, real grant reference, and exact read authorization and supported accounting interface are documented without inventing an endpoint or invoking an unapproved store route.
- A current existing grant matches installation.bootstrap-admission-contract-stage, its real local identity, exact finalized plan/source closure and decision, one bounded capability, parameters, transitive scope, issuance/expiry and revocation.
- Actual finite phase, parent, attempt, cleanup and evidence values, host-safe units/ranges, aggregate rules and permitted narrowing are evidenced; source-authoring limits are not mistaken for validation-launch authority.
- Authoritative current consumption, reservations, remaining attempts/time, original clock/deadline lineage, version and observation freshness are corroborated. Shared/nested consumption cannot be reset or enlarged by a new wrapper, task, boot, purpose or caller parameter.
- Accounting access and grant scope are separately verified. Missing, conflicting, stale, consumed, exhausted or unverifiable records remain BLOCKED; no default allowance is invented.
- No prerequisite is claimed satisfied until a complete current matching grant and accounting readback are verified. If unavailable, deliver an exact owner/source request and retain the blocker rather than claiming a captured budget.

## Out of scope

Issuing, renewing, consuming or reserving budgets; reading live coordinator accounting without route-specific permission; initialization or mutation of stores; direct SQL substitution for an unavailable approved route; allocation; installing accounting code; changing clocks, limits, counters, policy or shared documents; source staging; validation, imports, scripts, syntax checks, tests, builds, CI, services, signals, reclaim, cutover or deployment.

## Steps

1. **Bind the proposed subject** — Reconcile permitted documentation with the factual recheck and identify exact grant/accounting requirements for the same finalized source-stage subject. Keep historical report claims separate from current remaining-budget evidence.
2. **Locate authorized budget access** — Identify the authentic grant source and documented read route with explicit access scope, finite read bounds and stable versions. Request the smallest authorized export/read evidence if access is not already established, without opening the store.
3. **Assess actual accounting** — Within verified access only, compare grant values and current consumption/reservations with aggregate, narrowing, expiry/revocation and original clock lineage. Reject unsafe representations, enlarged limits and cross-domain borrowing without consuming any allowance.
4. **Publish an intake dossier** — Record source/permission references, effective and remaining values if actually verified, and FOUND/BLOCKED/UNKNOWN limitations in a separate tracked report. If a supported route is missing, propose its exact owner decision and integration scope without implementing or granting it.

## Task identity and coordination

Platform task acceptance and a plan file are not budget authority. Do not allocate local IDs. Evidence must bind the real source-issued identity and exact subject also used by approval intake; parallel collection does not waive that match.

Write only this task's report, not the shared capability locator, primary handoff, approval dossier, supervisor dossier, or live accounting. A later validation operation requires its own matching authorization and budget; a source-stage grant cannot be borrowed.

## Pre-existing failures to ignore

None authorized. Preserve raw failures, accounting conflicts and missing lineage without waiver, refund or reclassification.

## Validation

**Command:** None authorized; bounded permitted evidence readback only.
**Why:** Neither safe-shape constants nor a read-only API authenticate a current grant or authorize access.
**Do not escalate:** Unapproved accounting access, missing source provenance, unknown consumption, expiry, exhaustion, unsafe aggregates or clock mismatch means BLOCKED. Do not run a diagnostic to obtain budget evidence or reuse historical limits.
